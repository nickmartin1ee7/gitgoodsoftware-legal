GroupTravel privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

GroupTravel helps a group of friends meet up. While you choose to share, the people in your meetup see where you are and when you'll arrive. Sharing stops on its own when you arrive, when you tap Stop, or when the meetup ends.

GroupTravel is made by GitGoodSoftware ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the GroupTravel Android app (com.gitgoodsoftware.grouptravel) and the GroupTravel browser join page collect, why, who it goes to, and when it is deleted.

The short version

  • We only collect your location while you are sharing in a meetup, and only after you tap to start.
  • The Android app uses the "While using the app" location permission. It never asks for background ("all the time") location. While you share, a notification and an in-app banner are always shown, each with a Stop button.
  • There are no accounts. You only enter a display name.
  • Your location is deleted when you arrive, when you stop, or when the meetup ends, whichever comes first. Everything about a meetup is deleted when it ends.
  • We don't sell your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in GroupTravel.
  • GroupTravel is for adults 18 and older.

1. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Display name (whatever you type; it doesn't need to be your real name) When you create or join a meetup So the people in your meetup know who is who
Meetup details: meetup name, destination, meet time and end time When an organizer creates a meetup To run the meetup, work out arrival times and know when to stop sharing
Precise location (or approximate location, if that is all you allow), with its accuracy, speed and time Only while you are sharing in a meetup To show your position to your meetup, estimate your arrival time and detect when you've arrived
Arrival information: your estimated arrival time and whether and when you arrived While you are sharing To show the group who is on the way and who is there
Meetup access code: a random code that lets your device take part in one meetup When you join To make sure only members of a meetup can send or see its locations. Our server stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of it
Technical data: IP address, time of request and the address requested, in our server logs Whenever the app or join page talks to our server To keep the service running, secure and free of abuse

How location works on Android. Sharing starts only when you tap a button such as "Share until I arrive". We show you what will be shared before Android asks for the location permission. While you share, a foreground service keeps sending your location with the screen off, and Android shows a notification saying so. GroupTravel does not request the background location permission. Sharing never starts on its own, for example after a restart.

How location works in a browser. If you join from the link in a browser (for example on an iPhone), your location is sent only while the GroupTravel page is open and on screen. Your browser asks for permission first.

You can join without sharing. "Join without sharing" lets you see the meetup without sending your location.

What we don't collect. We don't collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID or payment details. We don't use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and we don't collect your location when you aren't sharing.

2. How we use it

We use this data only to run GroupTravel: to show your meetup where its members are and when they'll arrive, to detect arrival, to stop sharing at the right time, and to keep the service secure. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, build profiles of you, or use it for any other purpose.

3. Who can see it

  • The people in your meetup. Anyone who joins the meetup from its invite link can see the display names in it and, while you share, your location, arrival estimate and arrival status. Only share the link with people you want to see this. The organizer can remove a member, which stops that person's sharing and deletes their data from the meetup.
  • Service providers that work for us. They process data only on our instructions, to provide their service to us:
    • Stadia Maps (routing). To calculate arrival times, our server sends Stadia Maps your location rounded to about 11 metres, plus the destination. We never send your name, your IP address or any meetup or member ID with it, and your device never contacts Stadia Maps directly. Stadia Maps keeps its request logs for about 7 to 14 days and never more than 30 days. We have a data processing agreement with Stadia Maps.
    • Cloudflare (map display). The map images (from Protomaps, using OpenStreetMap data) are delivered through Cloudflare. To load them, your device contacts Cloudflare directly, so Cloudflare receives your IP address and the area of the map you are looking at, but not your GPS location.
    • Our hosting provider, which runs our server and database.
  • When the law requires it. We may disclose data if we are legally required to, or to protect people's safety. Because we delete meetup data quickly, we usually have very little to disclose.

Google Play, which distributes the app, is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

4. How long we keep it

Sharing stops at the first of these: you arrive, you tap Stop, or the meetup ends. A meetup ends 60 minutes after its meet time, unless the organizer changes that.

  • Your location is kept only as your latest position, not a history of where you've been. It is deleted when you tap Stop, when the organizer removes you, and when the meetup ends.
  • When you arrive, sharing stops. You have 10 minutes to tap "I'm not there yet" if the app got it wrong. After that (or right away if you tap Done), your location is deleted and only the time you arrived is kept, until the meetup ends.
  • When the meetup ends, we delete all of its locations, arrival estimates, display names, access codes and meetup details.
  • Server logs are kept for no more than 14 days.
  • On your phone, the app keeps its settings and access codes until you clear its data or uninstall it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Choose "Join without sharing", or deny the location permission. You can still see the meetup.
  • Tap Stop in the app or in the notification at any time.
  • Change or remove the location permission in your phone's or browser's settings.
  • Uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Ask us to access or delete your data early by emailing contact@gitgoodsoftware.com. Since there are no accounts, please include the meetup name, your display name and roughly when it took place, so we can find it. We will reply within 30 days. Most data is deleted automatically when the meetup ends anyway.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to or restrict how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to use any of these rights. Where these laws apply, we process your data to provide the service you asked for, on the basis of your consent for location (which you can withdraw at any time by tapping Stop), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (server logs). We do not sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.

6. Security

All data between the app or browser and our server is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access codes are stored only in hashed form, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, which is one reason we keep so little and delete it quickly.

7. Age requirement

GroupTravel is intended only for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. Don't use GroupTravel if you are under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has provided data to us, we will delete it. Contact us if you believe this has happened.

8. Where data is processed

We and our service providers may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries where they operate. Where data moves between countries, we rely on our providers' data protection agreements and standard safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, for example a new kind of data or a new recipient, we will also tell you in the app before it applies.
